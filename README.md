# AI Prompt Logger

A browser extension that automatically captures and saves your prompts across AI tools — locally on your device.

## Supported Platforms
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- DeepSeek
- Google AI Studio

## Features
- **Live capture** — logs prompts as you type and submit
- **History scan** — scrape all prompts from any open chat
- **Download** — export as `.txt` or `.json` per platform or all at once
- **100% local** — all data stays in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere

---

## Installation (Chrome / Edge)

> No account or payment needed. Takes ~30 seconds.

1. **Download this extension**
   - Click the green **Code** button on this page → **Download ZIP**
   - Extract the ZIP to a folder on your computer

2. **Open your browser's extension page**
   - Chrome: go to `chrome://extensions`
   - Edge: go to `edge://extensions`

3. **Enable Developer Mode**
   - Toggle **Developer mode** ON (top-right corner)

4. **Load the extension**
   - Click **Load unpacked**
   - Select the folder you extracted in step 1

5. **Done!**
   - The 🧠 icon will appear in your toolbar
   - Visit ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, or AI Studio and start prompting

---

## How to Use

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Shows prompts captured in real-time from your current session |
| **History** | Click "Scan Current Chat" to save all prompts from an open chat |
| **Download** | Export your prompts as `.txt` or `.json` |
| **Settings** | Clear live prompts, history, or everything |

---

## Privacy

All data is stored using `chrome.storage.local` — it never leaves your browser. No servers, no accounts, no tracking.
